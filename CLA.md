# Contributor agreement status

**Drafting notice: contribution intake is closed. This is not an operative contributor agreement and must not be signed or relied upon.**

Before contributions open, counsel must complete and approve separate individual and entity forms based on the Harmony Contributor License Agreement version 1.0. The adopted forms must:

1. name the correct legal project steward and submission instructions;
2. use the copyright **license** form rather than the copyright assignment form;
3. expressly select outbound Option Five and remove every mutually exclusive outbound option;
4. preserve the Option Five promise that an accepted contribution also remains available under the license used for the material when submitted;
5. resolve the optional media-license field rather than leaving a placeholder;
6. preserve contributor representations, patent language, reservation of rights, and applicable individual/entity distinctions;
7. explain electronic assent and maintain an auditable acceptance record; and
8. be reviewed alongside chain of title, employment/prior-inventions obligations, and the planned future assignment to any LitScenes corporation.

The authoritative Harmony templates and adoption guide are at:

- <https://www.harmonyagreements.org/agreements>
- <https://www.harmonyagreements.org/use>
- <https://www.harmonyagreements.org/guide.html>

No pull request or other submission is accepted under a DCO-only process. A DCO does not provide the commercial relicensing grant this dual-license structure requires.
