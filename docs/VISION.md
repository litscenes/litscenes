# LitScenes Desktop Vision

LitScenes is a local-first filmmaking system for macOS that helps people turn personal media archives into coherent, story-rich video compositions longer than a prompt.

It establishes meaning and structure before rendering pixels: understand the media, articulate the story's purpose, cast recurring people and places, plan frames and scenes, then shape shots, cuts, narration, sound, and final output without losing provenance.

The product thesis is that finite meaning primitives, finite story primitives, a personal media archive, and contextual interpretation can produce infinite story compositions.

The north star is whether LitScenes can explain what each frame literally shows, the archetypal situation and symbols it implies, the value tension and thought it expresses, the theme and transformation it supports, and the beat it should serve.

Done means a creator can move from their own media and intent to a durable multi-scene story while retaining creative control, local ownership, honest provider costs, inspectable inference provenance, and recoverable artifacts throughout the process.
