# LitScenes Desktop Requirements

LitScenes Desktop is the public, canonical macOS application for turning personal media into story-aware planning, generation, editing, and export workflows.

## Desktop Go onboarding

Personal provider keys are the default setup. Go memberships are retained behind an explicit server feature flag; missing flags or failed configuration disable managed purchases and new generation. App Store purchases remain unavailable unless their existing readiness checks also pass. The following Go purchase requirements apply only when memberships are enabled.

- One anonymous public configuration request supplies membership availability and broadcast announcements. Fetch at launch and on foreground after five minutes, coalesce concurrent requests and use a five-second timeout. Startup, local editing and personal provider use never wait for this response; enabled state is not cached across launches.
- While disabled, Welcome and the default API keys Settings tab show expanded personal-key setup. Keep Advanced providers; hide Go plan, balance, refill and funding choices and skip automatic account/StoreKit activity. New work uses personal credentials; preserved managed intents and captured billing snapshots must not silently switch payer or submit while disabled.
- Announcements appear in the existing Notices tray independently of membership. Opening the tray acknowledges displayed stable IDs locally; new IDs restore its unread dot, and read announcements remain visible while published. Preserve storage notices and legacy notice documents. Announcement bodies are plain text and optional links require HTTPS.

- Account & usage opens once per launch until a personal OpenAI key or server-confirmed subscription is configured. Dismissal permits exploration for that launch; an existing project, unrelated vendor key, or dismissed Welcome Journey does not finish setup. Confirmed setup survives connection failures without authorizing paid access.
- Present Go, Go Plus, and **Self serve: use your own vendors**, with direct vendor billing clearly stated. Self-serve setup presents a two-key studio — OpenAI (media analysis, stories, frames) and FAL (video and image stacks) as co-heroes, with ElevenLabs optional directly below for voice — and keeps additional credentials and compatible endpoint/model overrides in Advanced providers. Hosted graph credentials are absent from setup; saved personal configuration remains intact.
- Go tiers are $16.80/month for 750 credits and $68/month for 3,500 credits, with the same capabilities. Monthly credits expire at renewal without rollover or automatic overages; preserve historical prepaid balances and use monthly credits first. Show taxes and authoritative native pricing where applicable. Desktop, SMS, and mobile balances are separate.
- Active paid subscribers can buy direct-download refills of 1,000 credits for $25 or 10,000 for $238, plus applicable tax. Show these in Account & usage and credit-exhaustion recovery, not initial onboarding. Refills require explicit confirmation, never expire, are spent after monthly credits, and do not change subscription renewal or the selected billing source. Refill actions appear only when the gateway supports them and the account is eligible; do not offer direct refill checkout in App Store builds. Preserve pending purchase identity and previously approved work through payment recovery.
- The purchase contract supports Stripe Managed Payments for direct downloads and StoreKit for native builds. Preview upgrade charges and credits before confirmation; apply upgrades after payment and downgrades/cancellation at renewal. Keep provider-specific billing management available during generation suspension. Receipt verification and recovery must preserve account and product ownership.
- Managed workflows require a server quote and maximum credit approval before generation. Queued/running work retains its captured prompt, settings, payer, account, and retry identity. Do not automatically resubmit an uncertain provider request or silently switch payers. Existing media and local import, browsing, editing, and export remain available without a subscription.
- Connection failures offer a useful explanation and retry. Unknown balances stay unknown. Every launch may read public configuration without account or project data; account reconnection and StoreKit activity require enabled memberships. Choosing a Go option checks current availability. Switching to self serve does not cancel subscription renewal.
- Save validated outputs durably before acknowledgment. Preserve recovery through interrupted project saves and expired server copies. Signed media transfers must not expose account credentials or persist signed capabilities. Fit/pad references without cropping and normalize output to the requested canvas.
- Expose only executable provider routes. Shared managed generation and reference editing use the managed image adapter; unsupported operations require a supporting personal provider. See [Desktop Go integration](desktop-go-integration.md) for account, native-purchase, provider, and recovery contracts.

## Product Contract

- The app is local-first. Projects, media indexes, credentials, prompts, traces, and generated artifacts remain on the operator's Mac except for provider requests the operator explicitly configures.
- An unconfigured Community install performs no background analytics or telemetry and remains usable with its bundled starter meaning and style resources.
- The primary workflow is MEDIA → STORY → CHARACTERS → SCENES. It should lead with usable content and direct next actions rather than internal pipeline terminology.
- Direct inference with the operator's own compatible key is a complete path. Optional hosted meaning retrieval may enrich it but must never be presented as required for the app to work.
- Provider/model/parameter controls must affect the next execution exactly as shown. Unavailable capabilities are visibly locked or omitted, never selectable theater.
- A provider refusal — credit exhaustion, a throttle persisting past bounded retries, a rejected credential, or an unreachable vendor — fails the requesting work immediately, carrying the provider's own explanation to the artifact that asked for it. No invisible hold and no automatic resubmission; a request whose paid acceptance is genuinely unknown may still pause for review instead of resubmitting. Key probes report a limited provider (HTTP 429) distinctly from a verified one.
- A failed media analysis marks the failed item itself with the plain-language cause; the run-level banner names the file but the diagnosis lives at the point of intent.

## Creative Workflow

- PLAY ALL prepares the complete selected Output sequence before autoplaying it from the beginning. A first cut finishing preparation must not start a partial reel that can stop before later cuts arrive. Cached cuts start without rebaking; failed, skipped or canceled cuts stay visible on the existing board as named retryable rows and the remaining playable cuts still run in order. The waiting notice counts ready cuts, and a reel playing fewer cuts than were picked marks its count. Export waits for the same local preparation, and closing or replacing the player invalidates pending playback callbacks.
- Restyle opens with No style. Choosing a style defaults each capable stack to Attach; single-image and unavailable image routes use Describe with Attach visibly disabled and explained. The executable route must carry both style and source images, preserve the source, reserve native style capacity, expose overflow, and persist the chosen mode and exact attachment provenance. Missing source/style media and impossible stale payloads require repair before spending.
- Desktop thumbnails show the complete image within their existing tile sizes, with neutral padding where needed. Circular and rounded badges preserve image corners; video samples fit within their temporal slots. Explicit crop/zoom tools and decorative backdrops retain their distinct functions.
- Add reference images includes Upload Images, reusing project image import. Imported records appear selected immediately, including in an empty library; Attach applies the ordered selection. Cancel leaves imports in the library without changing attached references. Duplicates, import progress, failures, and capacity warnings retain their existing semantics.

- The Story preview card shows up to four numbered scene titles in their existing order, retaining duplicate titles and the current navigation behavior.
- Analyze Media has an always-enabled Hide control that minimizes its log to a live status pill while analysis continues. Clicking the pill restores the same log; Cancel remains a separate action, and the pill can be closed once the run ends.
- Media intake preserves source identity and bytes, avoids accidental cropping in review surfaces, distinguishes missing files from managed/linked storage, and treats every source photo as an adoptable Frame without spend.
- STORY persists the creator's purpose, content type, constraints, cast, places, and source-media evidence. Saved state—not unsaved field text—determines readiness, and usable-but-imperfect story language may continue with warnings.
- CHARACTERS treats source images as inputs and rendered character sheets as continuity anchors. Rendering states, selected provider stack, source ordering, price, failure, staleness, and version choice remain visible and per-character.
- Character image creation and reference sheet generation are separate actions with independent persisted model choices. Image reference selection follows current sources until customized and displays the references actually used. A sheet requires a usable image reference, including an existing sheet; this is the approved workflow prerequisite, not a quality gate. New characters use manual sheet rendering while legacy effective preferences are preserved.
- Where hair is present, character refinement preserves established hair length, cut, silhouette, and texture across sheet panels and later updates unless the creator explicitly changes them; explicit changes apply consistently throughout the sheet. Conversation confirms saved instructions, never guarantees an uninspected image outcome. Source images and existing sheets provide identity evidence; the latest explicit creator instructions govern intended changes.
- Original-resolution character images and active or historical sheets open at actual pixel size in a large screen-relative inspector, with fit, actual-size, zoom and pan controls. Inspection does not change the selected continuity version; its version-strip badge reads Active.
- CHARACTERS supports deleting individual sheet versions from each thumbnail menu or right-click menu. Deleted versions stay out of browsing, pickers, and automatic character reference selection after reload or rescan; their files and provenance remain available to existing work. Deleting the active version selects the newest surviving version and restores its prompt hash; deleting the last clears the reference sheet. Surviving version numbers stay stable, and failed saves leave visible state unchanged.
- SCENES turns the saved story into concrete, diverse moments and Frames, preserves completed or attempted work across replanning, and makes planned intentions visibly different from queued or rendered artifacts.
- The SCENES thumbnail pool interleaves source photos and generated Frames newest first, including newly loading renders. Completion updates the same tile; adopted photos retain their source date and footage follows still images.
- Every Scene ends with one state-aware tail action: START SCENE when empty, ADD TO SCENE before playable video exists, and EXTEND SCENE afterward. AI, Frame, and Footage paths remain on this surface; animating an initial Frame is labeled distinctly from extending video. Opening or canceling AI continuation review must not mutate the Scene; confirmation shows the exact endpoint, executable method and model, duration/audio controls, and a complete price before any provider submission.
- AI Extend appends one continuation cell in the same Shot row without changing `shotId`, `activeRenderVersionId`, or numbered render history. Play, export, runtime, combination, and reel composition use the complete preserved source followed by the selected continuation clips. Only the confirmed link is generated.
- Each continuation owns immutable takes and their exact recipes. A successful tail retake selects automatically only while its reviewed source still matches the current cut; changed sources leave a ready, unselected take for review; a retake with downstream continuations waits for Use. Use changes the working selection at $0, preparing the current cut locally first when needed to preserve its edits. Dependent clips remain playable with stale warnings; separately priced Rechain repairs those dependencies and retains completed work if interrupted.
- Historical whole-Shot renders remain available as read-only previews. NEW VERSION makes an independent editable copy of a continuation-bearing Shot's selected sequence and edit state, sharing immutable media instead of dropping the continuation clips. Existing history remains on the source Shot.
- Continuation failures retain the safe phase and reason on the cell, take browser, Logs, and canonical traces. Completed provider output is persisted before local finishing. Repair uses retained media at $0; a new remote attempt requires price review. Unknown provider acceptance is distinguished from failure before submission.
- Continuation anchors may be ready Frames, ranged Footage, rendered Originals, selected continuation takes, or the current edited output. For edited video, local preparation includes picture order, OUT, cuts, Reverse, speed, loops, audio, and the active Look. Native Extend is offered only with an executable video context; paired-frame endings use supported models. Missing source media or an impossible composition requires repair in the same review.
- Every video-backed continuation uses the last displayed frame inside its source video or selected Footage range, with exact extraction timing and no assumed frame rate. New appends, retakes, Rechain and chain rebuilds share this rule. Retakes retain their recorded predecessor and show a correction notice when its verified ending differs from the historical input. Existing clips, inputs and history remain immutable.
- Endpoint caches are verified by extraction revision, source-content fingerprint, range and image fingerprint. Confirmation retains the reviewed image; missing, changed or unverified sources require reopening review before any provider submission. A missing generated video cannot silently become a still-start. Local preflight and endpoint events link into Logs with source/image fingerprints and requested/actual extraction timestamps.
- Shot-row thumbnails open their placement in the Shot modal paused; PLAY starts playback and must look enabled whenever saved media is playable. VIEW FRAME, ART DIRECT, TAKES, and INSPECT FOOTAGE remain explicit secondary row actions, with source inspection and take management available in the modal.
- The modal has explicit CURRENT, historical-version, and segment-preview states. The navigation highlight follows the viewed state; returning to CURRENT reloads the selected sequence even when its base media path matches a historical preview.
- Model provenance on the row and in the current modal describes selected sequence clips, including Footage, rather than the active whole-Shot render stamp. One model is named, two are both named, and larger mixtures show a model count. Ordered per-clip details remain inspectable separately from NEXT generation controls.
- A Frame appended after a completed continuation or readable rendered tail is a destination for an ending clip. RENDER ENDING… opens the same no-spend review from the row and player, immediately retaining the chosen ending Frame while preparing the exact current cut endpoint. It shows direction, executable paired-frame model, duration/audio settings, and full price; preparation failures keep the destination and direction with an exact reason and Refresh review. Extend Scene promotes a pending ending, and multiple staged destinations render in order. Confirmation generates only that connecting clip, preserving the existing Shot, earlier media, active render identity, and numbered history. The destination placement owns its takes; a completed ending can be extended again from its actual output endpoint.
- Ending takes persist immutable target Frame identity, image snapshot, and fingerprint with their recipe and source anchor. Native Extend and narration-only models are not selectable for paired-frame endings. Missing media is a repairable runtime prerequisite; merely having a historical render record does not make its source video available.
- Shot and cut editing is nondestructive and recoverable. Trims, skips, seams, loops, speed, picture insertions, narration, ambient audio, and version changes must preserve source media and support undo where the established workflow promises it.
- Export uses the assembled preview as authority, surfaces failures plainly, and never discards a completed provider or local artifact because a sibling operation failed.
- Selecting a new ending preserves preceding edits in an editable Earlier cut group, then appends the new video once outside that group. Earlier cut and Whole Shot controls use the existing editor, with explicit scope labels. All original segment cards, takes and immutable media remain accessible; Undo/Redo restores grouping and selection without deleting generated takes. Nested groups support repeated endings.
- Playback, export, combination and continuation preparation share the scoped composition. Disposable playback caches can rebuild from saved media and edits; a missing earlier cache must never make playback silently show only the later clips. Local preparation shows progress, or its error with Retry. Reviewed composition identity and source clips persist with continuation trace provenance; changed cuts require a fresh review before submission and prevent automatic selection at completion.

## Shot Editor

- The existing right-hand editor shows saved video for every current segment separately from its input Frames and next-render draft. Selected continuations, endings, preserved Originals, imported Footage, reused branch/combined clips and whole-video fallbacks remain inspectable; unavailable generation inputs never hide retained selected media.
- Selecting a saved segment card or Preview Clip plays the inspected take separately; Full Shot returns to the prior position. Row thumbnails focus, highlight and scroll to the corresponding editor card. Imported Footage previews its placed source range before any generated render.
- Every generated placement's retained takes are browsable on its card as a take strip: one thumb per distinct clip file across the Shot's render history (a clip reused verbatim by a later render is one take), the take in the film ringed, the previewed take marked, and a missing file named rather than hidden. Clicking a thumb previews that take in the player with one action bar naming the segment, take and whether it is in the film; ←/→ step takes and ↩/U puts the previewed take in the film. Selected and In film have separate labels. Compare is withheld from this workflow. Use is free, undoable and never changes render history. A newly rendered take of a placement plays at once; the previous take stays one click away. Continuation placements present the same strip from their record and keep the full browser for repair and rechain.
- Each segment card shows the inspected take’s thumbnail, saved model, duration, prompt and inputs. Next take is explicitly based on that take. Editable direction, timing authority, model, duration, audio and supported resolution belong to a durable draft keyed by placement and immutable take; switching takes and reopening preserves each draft independently. Use saved recipe restores that take’s saved recipe. Existing segment overrides initialize the in-film draft; unrendered segments inherit Shot defaults. Full Shot returns card inspection to the in-film takes without discarding alternate drafts. A draft is consumed when its render is dispatched: its recipe becomes the placement's saved overrides (what the in-film take seeds from) and its base take seeds from that take's own saved recipe again; unsent drafts on other takes survive. Unsupported saved recipes require an executable model choice before rendering.
- Improve and Suggest are small text-AI links beside Render new take in the existing segment editor. Improve clarifies the written action and camera direction while preserving intent; Suggest proposes a fresh direction from available Frame descriptions, relationships, narration and model/duration, ignoring existing text. Neither generates video. Revert restores the selected saved prompt or initial unrendered direction; Undo restores the previous text and timing authority.
- Assistance is traceable through the shared text workflow and inference store. Operators can keep typing while it runs. Changed text or segment inputs require Apply/Dismiss for the result; closing the editor prevents late application. Duplicate requests for one segment are prevented; failure keeps the draft and offers Retry.
- Untouched legacy timed direction remains executable and visible as compiled text. An explicit text edit or AI replacement saves text and raw authority atomically, retaining the old plan and immutable media. Native multi-shot timing changes are explained before rendering. Story Beats elsewhere are unaffected. Paid review uses readable dark ink on cream, including native controls, independently of the surrounding appearance.
- Render new take, Takes and Render Ending address their own placement. Opening a review flushes the current prompt and timing authority together, carries the exact base take, owner, original continuation anchor, direction, timing authority, executable recipe and resolution, and adds no attempt until confirmation. A failed draft save prevents paid review/submission using stale text and offers retry from the segment card. Failed or in-flight alternatives do not replace selected media.
- Extend Scene reuses the row's AI/Frame/Footage picker inside the modal. Frame Creator returns to that Shot. New Version opens a successfully persisted independent copy; changing Shot identity resets player selection and focus. Whole-chain regeneration is a secondary action with a complete estimate, distinct from generating one take.
- Render history is a read-only provenance plate; its Preview plays a render's saved whole-shot file under its own artifact identity and changes nothing. The player bar carries no version numerals: which take plays is decided per segment, and a render in flight is disclosed as showing the last finished render. Active Looks retain Edit Original. Choosing a narration model for the next run leaves current saved segments visible and explains the established whole-Shot narration workflow. The player labels saved whole-render previews separately from Full Shot. Copy Frame Pair retains the existing structural clipboard behavior.
- One action bar below the player owns Copy and Show in Finder. Both target the viewer: Copy Take N uses the inspected immutable take; Copy Saved Render uses the inspected history artifact; Copy Full Shot prepares the current edited film, including trim, arrangement, rate, reverse, loops, Look and mixed audio. Full Shot preparation is local, reuses a durable fingerprinted file, never invokes a paid provider and rejects results if the film or viewer changes. Duplicate card-level copy/reveal actions are removed. Paste Video at End on another Shot row or its thumbnail in the same project appends independent playable footage, retaining provenance and embedded audio; paste supports undo/redo and reopening. Missing files, project mismatch and structural locks explain why an action cannot run.
- Both direct and render-history Continuation Takes browsers synchronize inspection with the player and pass the previewed take into Render new take. Continuation Takes keeps readable dark ink on cream in both system appearances, including its sheet, row popover and confirmation dialog. Metadata uses at least 10-point text and prompts 11-point text; secondary and disabled labels stay legible, and selection is distinguished by both text and borders.
- Controls wrap as whole controls within the editor and timeline toolbars; action labels must not collapse into vertical fragments.

## Shot Narration

- Narration takes stay visible inline on the existing Shot row alongside the video render review. Each attempt retains its own identity, transcript, voice, audio, speed and outcome. Play auditions a take without selecting it; Use makes a ready take the active voice-over and is undoable. A failed or interrupted generation preserves the prior ready take. Legacy projects retain their actual saved recording without inventing audio from historical transcript strings.
- The next-video model picker remains available with narration present, including in the LTX review. Ordinary executable video models preserve narration as mixed voice-over; LTX uses the selected authored narration as provider input. Model changes preserve prompt drafts and existing media. LTX duration gates use exact 2–20-second bounds, show the actual duration and repair action, and do not restrict ordinary models. A changed take or timing warns when an existing LTX picture needs synchronization.
- Copy Narration transfers a whole take, including script, voice, current speed and provenance. Paste Narration works on empty, populated and collapsed Shot rows, through explicit actions and keyboard commands outside text editing. It creates an independent active take at zero while retaining destination history and mix, without provider or drafting calls. Source trim and placement are excluded. Media is copied into the destination project so source deletion or speed changes cannot alter the pasted take. Missing files leave destination state intact. Paste supports undo/redo.
- Copy in a narration timeline region offers both its complete identifiable take to Shot-row Paste Narration and the selected region, including its edits, to timeline Paste. Legacy region clipboards resolve through their source Shot and exact media reference; missing or ambiguous provenance explains how to copy again from Narration Takes. Copy confirmation follows a verified native clipboard write, including keyboard commands; Cut never deletes source audio after an unsuccessful copy. All rows refresh when clipboard contents change, and unavailable Paste actions explain the actual refusal rather than silently appearing disabled.
- Take audio remains intact when a Shot has no video or a shorter video. Previewing a take plays its full audio; the row warns that composed playback/export end with the video. Use retains destination placement/gain/mute and resets to the full selected take. Existing output scopes retain their saved audio selection and media; each local speed adjustment writes a separate derivative.
- Speech and script traces remain distinct and connected to durable takes. Started, saved, failed, canceled and interrupted outcomes are recorded in the shared trace store and linked into Logs. Provider-bound speech text and safe media hashes remain inspectable. Completed narration and speed work saves to its originating project after a workspace switch.

## Image Prompt Preservation and Shortening

- Preserve the complete editable prompt and distinguish authored, assembled, prepared and actually submitted text. Character prompt storage, camera direction, render-stack defaults and image adapters must never silently slice creative text. Existing renders remain unchanged; uncaptured historical stages stay unknown.
- Apply only verified provider endpoint, field and billing-route constraints after composition and actual reference selection. Count Unicode scalar values. Personal Nano Banana 2 accepts 50,000 characters; its Go route accepts 10,000. Outpaint accepts 500 including an empty prompt, Reve 2.1 accepts 4,000, MAI Image 2.5 accepts 5,000, Stability Ultra accepts 10,000 separately for prompt and negative prompt, and OpenAI GPT image create/edit accepts 32,000. Responses has a separate contract. Unknown endpoints and custom gateways receive no invented limit; legacy configured limits are retained only for compatibility.
- Only a verified over-limit image request invokes structured OpenAI shortening. The app retains original text and owns reference order, identifiers, filenames, roles, required layout and camera geometry. Rewriting receives the complete context and preserves meaning, explicit creator edits, unspecified identity and negative constraints. Source images, reference sheets and composites have distinct purposes. Final reference instructions must describe actual attachments; omissions remain visible.
- Shortening permits one initial response plus one semantic correction, validates the complete reassembled fields locally and never falls back to clipping. Invalid output, unavailable text service, cancellation or fixed structure that cannot fit stops before image submission and offers editing, another model or retry. Inputs within their limits make no shortening call. A valid compatible preparation survives retry and restart; changing text, references, model, endpoint, route or constraints invalidates reuse.
- Go's existing image-spend confirmation discloses actual counts and offers See what changed before approval. Personal-provider and automatic chat rendering show the same notice without pausing. Authored text stays editable and unchanged; before/after comparison and stage details remain available in existing Character, Activity and Logs surfaces after success or failure. Existing text-spend approvals and uncertain-submission protections remain in force.
- Persist safe prompt stages, rewrite outputs and attempts, bindings, trace relationships, provider request identifiers and failure details through the canonical inference store. A generic provider 422 is not evidence of prompt length or unsafe content. Explicit structured length rejections supply route-scoped evidence for a user-initiated retry; they never trigger automatic image resubmission. Chat distinguishes saved instructions from a completed render and clears rendering status on terminal outcomes.
- This contract covers still-image workflows. Video prompt contracts remain separate. Implementation is in the current working tree; commit hash pending explicit authorization. The preservation diff was built and validated independently before the shortening diff; see `plans/20260925T175019Z-image-prompt-preservation.md` and its work log.

## Data, Inference, and Spend

- Project-local SQLite is the canonical structured state store. Persisted-format changes use explicit, tolerant migrations and stable project/artifact identities.
- Repeated text, image, video, audio, enrichment, evaluation, and agent workflows are incomplete until their full safe lifecycle is written to the shared inference trace store and inspectable through the established trace tooling.
- Traces preserve the exact provider-bound prompt, source/operator prompt when different, stable run and parent/group identity, workflow step, artifact identity, provider/model/parameters, request/response identifiers, status, latency, errors, usage/cost when available, parsed output, and safe media references or hashes.
- Secrets, access tokens, signed URLs, raw PII, and unnecessary binary media never enter logs or traces.
- Paid actions state their provider and expected price before submission, create durable spend/provenance records, and distinguish unpriced work from free work. Retrying a remote stage must not silently spend again.
- Continuation generation, Rechain, and generated-chain rebuild refuse provider submission when any requested link lacks a complete estimate. Every completed continuation clip remains reusable after later failure; retry resumes from unresolved work rather than regenerating successful links.

## Concurrent Work and Operational History

- Selecting another project changes the visible workspace without canceling or retargeting work. Each visited project retains its engine, selections, and unfinished view drafts. Refreshing the project registry must not tear down creative state.
- Independent Frames, Shots, character studies/sheets, narration and saved-output exports can proceed together. Busy indicators and mutation conflicts belong to the affected artifact. Shared queues admit up to eight image, three video, three text, two audio and two local jobs; request and encoder limits also constrain workflows that fan out.
- Confirmed requests are persisted before provider submission. Frame Creator closes after its submitted batch has been durably accepted. Repeated clicks on the same in-flight request must not spend again. Completion reconciles with the owning project's current documents and retains completed sibling artifacts.
- LOGS lives in a compact utility navigation at the far right of the main navigation bar. Its nonmodal panel opens beneath that bar, occupies approximately 60% of window width and 90% of available height, and leaves uncovered workspace interactive. Close and Escape dismiss it.
- Logs defaults to all projects and preserves filters across project switches and closing/reopening. Project, status, provider, workflow and text searches query durable history. Active work appears first; older history loads in pages. Details expose steps, safe requests/prompts, model and provider identifiers, and linked spend estimates when recorded. Unknown cost remains unknown. Saved Scene Plan versions remain accessible under a project filter.
- Operational jobs/events share the canonical inference SQLite database in every build. Higher-level jobs group repeated steps; older inference clients receive a request-level job through the shared transport. Recording finalization, imports/scans, local exports, and inference workflows produce structured events rather than depending on scraped console output. Activity reads the same job lifecycle.
- Manual generation Pause is removed; playback and recording Pause remain. Quit/crash and vendor failures create explicit automatic pauses. Safe retrieval/rejected-request retries are bounded; funding and authentication failures pause the affected vendor queue immediately while unrelated vendors and local work continue.
- Continue is executable for a retained rejected request and refreshes its existing authentication scheme from current credentials. Queued work can be canceled; running jobs offer Stop after this step where remote cancellation is unavailable. Existing artifact-specific cancel/resume controls retain their provider semantics.
- Relaunch does not automatically recreate a paid submission. Known resumable Look jobs use existing provider request identifiers; ambiguous acceptance requires checking the saved request and provider before retry. Other interrupted jobs retain their saved request and open the original artifact for review of current inputs, capabilities and pricing. Completed media can use the existing local repair paths.
- Resetting a project's creative database is blocked while that project has unfinished operational jobs, because background completion could repopulate or corrupt reset state. This destructive-state prerequisite does not block unrelated projects.

AI continuation and standalone Footage placements already represent video and are not duplicated. Saved videos replace their loading state in place; retakes use the same tile and keep current playback available.

Interrupted work never keeps spinning after relaunch.

Expanded Inputs, Result/Failure, Activity, and Technical details expose safe prompts, source Frames where recorded, saved media, provider requests, and copyable diagnostics. ## Validation and Compatibility

- Hard failures are limited to invalid required persisted state, unavailable credentials/services, security or privacy risk, destructive operations, and impossible runtime prerequisites. Creative quality concerns remain soft warnings with repair, regenerate, review, or continue actions.
- Fixture-specific entities never become production inference rules. Generic behavior must survive renamed entities and materially different projects.
- Existing project documents decode tolerantly. New schemas are closed and versioned where strict provider output is required; all model-generated structured outputs validate before persistence.
- Saving a Shot publishes the exact canonical document successfully persisted. Append, ending and take selection preserve earlier selected media, placement order and numbered render history through save/reopen; normalization never silently drops a continuation-owned or referenced entry. A pair-take selection is stored only as the chosen clip file per placement; a selection whose file no retained render or seed carries falls back to the playable render silently, a continuation-owned placement never carries one, and activating a finished render clears only the selections of placements it freshly rendered.
- Continuation and ending attempts must be saved before provider submission. A persistence failure cannot report generation success; completed local media retains a recovery recipe for repair without another provider call.
- Legacy missing continuation ancestors recover once from retained take ancestry. Related extension-only historical outputs appear as alternate Takes, retaining original artifacts and provider provenance; ordinary editing never reconstructs deliberately removed entries.
- Combined source boundaries begin independent sequences. Their first Frames cannot become ending targets for the previous source's continuation.
- AI continuation identity survives save/reload. Previously cleared markers recover from their continuation records without changing media or converting destination Frames into open-ended extensions.
- Historical AI-extension clips migrate deterministically into continuation take records without copying or converting media. Legacy adjacent rendered links retain lineage and tail-clip provenance; in-flight attempts reconcile as interrupted after relaunch.
- The supported development baseline is macOS 15+, Xcode 16.3+, and Swift 6.1+. The repository must pass its Swift build, full existing test suite, source-hygiene checks, and REUSE licensing validation.
